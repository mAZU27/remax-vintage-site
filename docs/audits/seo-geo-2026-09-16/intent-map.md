## Content decisions

No competing recruitment page, new buy/sell slug or thin neighbourhood page was created. Foz, Boavista and Cedofeita are already discussed by the site; their mention does not assert available listings or measured demand. A later local guide must have a named reviewer, verifiable transport/amenity sources, original agency knowledge, real photos with rights and a dated review. Omit price/demand statistics unless the data definition and date can be supported.

Suggested draft briefs, held until agency input:

1. “Comprar na Foz do Douro: critérios a discutir com o seu consultor” — building condition, accessibility, parking and proximity preferences; concrete verified local examples, no blanket price claims.
2. “Mudar de carreira para o imobiliário no Porto” — actual collaboration model, onboarding, costs and support; careers page remains the application destination.
3. “Preparar a primeira conversa para vender casa” — property facts to gather and questions to ask; legal document requirements reviewed by a qualified agency professional.

Only publish a standalone guide when it adds useful depth beyond the existing service FAQ. Otherwise improve the existing page.
